### Synthetic Example: Weighting of Toy Index Components

This public demo fragment is a synthetic teaching example. It is not copied from a proprietary methodology.

On each Selection Day, each Index Component is assigned a weight based on its Float Market Capitalization subject to the following constraints:

- Each region must represent exactly 50% of the total Index
- The weight of any single Index Component is capped at 5%.

The weights are redistributed proportionally in an iterative process until both constraints are satisfied.
